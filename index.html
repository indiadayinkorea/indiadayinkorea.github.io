About the Event

A Celebration of Two Great Nations

India Day 2026 brings the vibrant culture, rich traditions, and dynamic spirit of India to the banks of the Han River. Hosted by the Embassy of India in Korea, this landmark event marks the deepening bonds of friendship between India and the Republic of Korea.

From classical dance and music to culinary delights and art exhibitions, the day offers an immersive journey through India's magnificent heritage — shared with warmth alongside our Korean friends and the global community in Seoul.

🇮🇳 🇰🇷
Partnership Since 1973 Over five decades of diplomatic, economic, and cultural ties between India and Korea.

The Tiranga — India's national flag, with the Ashoka Chakra representing the eternal wheel of law and virtue

Programme

A Day Full of Wonder

From morning ceremonies to a starlit riverside finale — a full day celebrating India's heritage.

🌅

10:00 AM

Inaugural Ceremony

Official opening with flag hoisting, national anthems of India and Korea, and welcome addresses by dignitaries.

💃

11:00 AM

Classical Dance Showcase

Bharatanatyam, Kathak, and Odissi performances by acclaimed dancers from India and the Indian diaspora in Korea.

🍛

12:00 PM

Culinary Festival

A grand food bazaar featuring regional cuisines — from Punjabi curries to Kerala seafood and Rajasthani sweets.

🎨

1:00 PM

Art & Craft Exhibition

Mehndi, rangoli, block printing, and live pottery — interactive workshops open to all guests.

🎵

3:00 PM

Music & Bollywood

Live Bollywood music, classical instrumental performances, and a special Indo-Korean fusion concert.

🎆

6:00 PM

Gala Evening

A grand closing reception with cultural awards, fashion showcase, and a spectacular riverside finale.

Experience India

Culture in Every Corner

India Day is a sensory celebration — every detail a window into 5,000 years of civilization.

🕌

Heritage & History

Photo exhibitions and documentary screenings tracing India's ancient civilizations, independence movement, and modern rise.

👗

Fashion & Textiles

Silk sarees, handloom weaves, and embroidered ensembles — a fashion walk celebrating India's breathtaking textile traditions.

🏏

Sports & Games

Cricket exhibition, kabaddi demonstrations, and traditional Indian board games — fun for the whole family.

🪔

Spiritual Traditions

Yoga demonstrations, meditation sessions, and an Ayurveda wellness corner for holistic discovery.

🎬

Cinema & Media

Flowers & Festivity

Marigold installations, jasmine garland workshops, and vibrant rangoli art created live throughout the day.
